The old problem, solved the old way — by speaking

Typing an order in English is slow. Calling the shop means waiting, writing it down, and remembering prices. This agent does all three, by voice.

🗣️ Customer bolta hai

Hinglish me, jaise ghar par baat karte ho. English type karne ki zaroorat nahi.

🧠 Agent samajhta hai

Gnani STT sunta hai, agent sirf dukaan ki price list se rate nikalta hai. Rate guess nahi hota.

🔊 Bolkar confirm karta hai

Gnani TTS Hinglish me total bolkar sunata hai, taaki galat order pakda ja sake.

🧑‍🌾 Dukandar accept karta hai

Order dashboard par aata hai. Ek tap me accept ya reject, kagaz par likhne ki zaroorat nahi.

How it works

1
Speak. Customer phone ya browser par Hinglish me order bolta hai.
2
Listen. Gnani Prisma STT usko text banata hai, shor wali audio par bhi.
3
Match & total. Agent price list se item dhoondhta hai, total nikalta hai, aur unknown item par haath nahi uthata — clarification poochhta hai.
4
Confirm & handoff. Gnani Timbre TTS bolkar confirm karta hai. Customer “haan” bole to order save, “nahi” bole to cancel, mushkil case me dukandar ko handoff.
