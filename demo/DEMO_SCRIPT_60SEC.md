# 60-second demo video script — Bolkar Order Karo

Target award: Demo Day Drop (best 60-second video). Keep it one continuous
screen + voice recording. No fake calls, no real personal data.

## Shot plan

- **0-10 sec — Speak:** Face the camera / mic and say clearly in Hinglish:
  "Bhaiya, 2 kg atta aur 1 litre tel chahiye, kal subah de dena."
  Show the mic recording on screen.
- **10-25 sec — Transcript + match:** Show the Gnani transcript, then the
  matched items from the price list and the total (Rs 240 in the demo list).
  Say: "Gnani STT ne Hinglish samjha, aur price list se rate mila — koi
  andaza price nahi."
- **25-35 sec — Voice confirmation:** Play the Gnani TTS confirmation back:
  "Aapka order: 2 kg atta, 1 litre tel. Total 240 rupaye."
- **35-45 sec — Shopkeeper side:** Show the order appearing on the dashboard.
  Tap Accept. Say: "Dukandar ko ek tap me order mil gaya."
- **45-60 sec — Hard case:** Play one noisy / phone-like test, show what the
  agent got wrong or asked again, and the human handoff message.
  End line: "Happy path sab dikhate hain. Humne mushkil audio bhi dikhaya."

## On-screen text (keep short)

1. "Hinglish voice order"
2. "Gnani Prisma STT + Timbre TTS"
3. "No invented prices"
4. "Shopkeeper dashboard + human handoff"
